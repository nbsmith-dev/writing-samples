01 — Overview

About this guide

Scope, audience, and organization of this installation guide.

This guide describes how to unpack, mount, connect, and power on the VSG-100 Secure Network Gateway. Use it to bring a new VSG-100 unit into service, before you perform any security configuration.

This guide is for personnel who install and maintain network equipment in a secured facility. It assumes you're familiar with standard rack-mount hardware and basic networking cabling, but it doesn't assume prior experience with the VSG-100 specifically.

This guide covers:

  • Site and environmental requirements
  • Unpacking and inspecting the unit
  • Mounting the VSG-100 in an equipment rack
  • Connecting power and network cabling
  • Powering on and verifying basic operation

This guide doesn't cover security configuration. After you complete installation, refer to the VSG-100 System Security Plan for procedures covering access control and boundary protection configuration.

03 — Task

Unpack and inspect the VSG-100

Unpack the VSG-100 and confirm the shipment is complete and undamaged before you install it.

Before you begin

Clear a flat, static-safe work surface near the installation site.

Caution:The VSG-100 contains components sensitive to electrostatic discharge (ESD). Wear an ESD wrist strap, and keep the unit on an antistatic mat until it's installed in the rack.

Procedure

  1. Inspect the shipping carton for visible damage before opening it.

    If the carton is damaged, photograph it before opening, and contact your shipping provider.

  2. Open the carton, and remove the packing materials.

    Note:Retain all packaging until the unit passes verification in Power on and verify the VSG-100. You'll need the original packaging for any warranty return.

  3. Confirm the shipment contains the following items:

    • One VSG-100 Secure Network Gateway unit
    • One AC power cord
    • One rack-mount kit (two rails, mounting screws)
    • Quick start card

  4. Inspect the unit for physical damage, including the chassis, connectors, and mounting ears.

    Restriction:Don't power on a unit that shows signs of physical damage. Contact your equipment supplier instead.

  5. Record the unit's serial number, printed on the underside of the chassis, in your asset inventory.

Result: you've confirmed the shipment is complete and the unit is undamaged, and you're ready to prepare the installation site.

04 — Task

Mount the VSG-100 in an equipment rack

Install the VSG-100 in a standard 19-inch equipment rack using the supplied rack-mount kit.

Before you begin

  • Confirm the site meets the requirements in Site and environmental requirements
  • Rack-mount kit (supplied)
  • Phillips screwdriver
  • A second person to help support the unit's weight during installation
Warning:Confirm the rack is secured and stable, with anti-tip supports engaged if the rack isn't permanently fixed to the floor. A rack that shifts or tips during installation can cause serious injury.
Caution:The VSG-100 requires two people to lift safely. Lifting it alone risks personal injury and can drop and damage the unit.

Procedure

  1. Attach a mounting rail to each side of the rack at the height you've selected for the VSG-100.

    Use two mounting screws per rail, at minimum.

  2. With a second person supporting the opposite side, lift the VSG-100 into place on the rails.

    Caution:Keep fingers clear of the gap between the chassis and the rail while positioning the unit, to avoid pinch injury.

  3. Align the mounting ears with the rack's mounting holes.

  4. Secure the unit to the rack using the supplied mounting screws, two per side.

  5. If your rack is a four-post rack, attach a rear support bracket to the VSG-100's rear support bracket mounting holes.

    See VSG-100 rear panel to locate these mounting holes. A rear support bracket isn't required on a two-post rack.

  6. Confirm the unit is level and firmly seated before releasing support.

Result: the VSG-100 is mounted and ready for power and network connections.

05 — Task

Connect power to the VSG-100

Connect the VSG-100 to a power source without powering it on.

Before you begin

  • The VSG-100 mounted in the rack (see Mount the VSG-100 in an equipment rack)
  • Supplied AC power cord
  • A dedicated, grounded power circuit
Warning:Electric shock hazard. Confirm the power circuit is properly grounded, and don't connect the VSG-100 to power with wet hands or in a wet environment.
Caution:Confirm the power source matches the VSG-100's rated input (100-240 VAC, 50-60 Hz) before connecting. Connecting an unrated power source can damage the unit.

Procedure

  1. Confirm the VSG-100's power switch, if equipped, is in the off position.

  2. Connect an approved grounding strap from the rack's grounding bus to the chassis ground lug on the VSG-100 rear panel.

    See VSG-100 rear panel to identify the ground lug.

  3. Connect the AC power cord to the power connector on the VSG-100 front panel.

    See VSG-100 front panel to identify the power connector.

  4. Route the power cord along the rack's cable management arm, away from network cabling.

    Caution:Don't block the cooling fan exhaust vents on the VSG-100 rear panel with cabling. Restricted airflow can cause the unit to overheat.

  5. Connect the other end of the power cord to the dedicated power circuit.

  6. Don't power on the unit yet.

    Important:Complete Connect network interfaces on the VSG-100 before you power on the VSG-100. Powering on before network connections are in place can cause the unit to come up in an unexpected state.

Result: the VSG-100 has power available, and you're ready to connect network cabling.

06 — Task

Connect network interfaces on the VSG-100

Connect the VSG-100's Interface A and Interface B ports to the tactical network segment and the program office network.

Before you begin

  • The VSG-100 mounted and connected to power (not yet powered on)
  • Two shielded network cables rated for your network's speed
  • Confirmed port assignments on the tactical network segment and the program office network

Interface A connects to the tactical network segment. Interface B connects to the program office network. See VSG-100 front panel to locate both ports.

Important:Connecting a cable to the wrong interface can expose CUI to an unauthorized network segment. Confirm each cable's origin against your approved network diagram before you connect it.
Caution:The network connectors are sensitive to electrostatic discharge. Continue to observe ESD precautions while making these connections.

Procedure

  1. Connect one network cable from the tactical network segment to Interface A on the VSG-100 front panel.

  2. Connect a second network cable from the program office network to Interface B on the VSG-100 front panel.

  3. Route both cables along the rack's cable management arm, and label each cable at both ends.

    Consistent labeling makes later maintenance and troubleshooting faster.

  4. Confirm both cable connections are fully seated.

    Note:A cable that isn't fully seated can cause an intermittent link, which is difficult to diagnose after the unit is in service.

Result: the VSG-100 is connected to both networks, and you're ready to power on the unit.

07 — Task

Power on and verify the VSG-100

Power on the VSG-100, and confirm it starts up correctly before you begin security configuration.

Before you begin

  • The VSG-100 mounted, powered, and connected to both networks
  • Completed Connect network interfaces on the VSG-100
Warning:If you notice smoke, an unusual smell, or an unusual sound while powering on the VSG-100, disconnect power immediately, and don't reconnect it. Contact your equipment supplier before further use.

Procedure

  1. Turn on the VSG-100 using the front-panel power switch, if equipped, or by confirming power is applied.

  2. Watch the PWR LED. Confirm it turns solid green within 10 seconds.

    If the PWR LED doesn't turn on, confirm the power connection from Connect power to the VSG-100.

  3. Watch the SYS LED during startup. Confirm it changes from blinking to solid green within approximately 90 seconds.

    A solid green SYS LED indicates the unit has completed startup and is ready for configuration.

  4. Confirm the ALM LED is off.

    Caution:If the ALM LED is lit, don't proceed to security configuration. Refer to the VSG-100 maintenance manual for alarm troubleshooting.

  5. Confirm link activity on both Interface A and Interface B.

    Each interface should show a steady link indication once its connected network segment is active.

Result: the VSG-100 has started successfully and is ready for initial configuration. Refer to the VSG-100 System Security Plan for the access control and boundary protection procedures that follow installation.
