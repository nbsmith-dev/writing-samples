General Information

This repository collects representative technical writing samples across four skill areas: structured authoring (S1000D IETMs, DITA topic sets), standards compliance (DID/CDRL and CUI-aware documentation practice), editing craft (Simplified Technical English and plain-language reference tools), and systems & process thinking (documentation change control, governance workflows, and engineering literacy) — drawn from 15+ years writing DoD- and industrial-compliant documentation for programs including the RQ-4 Global Hawk, EMALS/Ford-class carriers, and commercial aircraft maintenance publications.

Sample — Hypothetical / Non-Proprietary

Every sample below was authored independently by Nathan Smith as a demonstration of technical writing method and format conformance. None contain classified, export-controlled, or proprietary data — all part numbers, drawings, and technical values are fictional. Each PDF carries its own disclaimer on the cover page.
